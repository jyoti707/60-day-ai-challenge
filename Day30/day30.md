# Day 30 – Build a Supply Chain Optimizer

## Overview
Built a single-file React-based Supply Chain Builder that teaches supply chain concepts through an interactive simulation. The application explains every business decision in simple language and updates performance metrics after each choice.

## Features
- Beginner-friendly supply chain lessons
- Random company generation
- Supplier selection
- Factory location planning
- Warehouse strategy
- Transportation selection
- Inventory management
- Live business metrics
- Final optimization dashboard
- Replay with a new company

## My Results
- Overall Supply Chain Score: **44/100**

### Final Metrics
- Cost: 80
- Delivery Speed: 50
- Risk: 45
- Customer Satisfaction: 68
- Sustainability: 15

## Biggest Optimization Decision
My supply chain decisions prioritized operational efficiency but resulted in higher costs and very low sustainability. This demonstrated that optimizing one area often creates trade-offs in others, making balance the key to a strong supply chain.

## Key Learnings
- Every supply chain decision affects multiple business metrics.
- Multiple suppliers reduce dependency but can increase complexity.
- Transportation choices balance speed, cost, and environmental impact.
- Inventory strategies directly influence customer satisfaction and operational costs.
- Successful supply chains require balancing cost, speed, risk, and sustainability.
