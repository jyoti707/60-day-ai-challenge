# Verse Companion — 10-Day Capstone

🚀 **Day 1 of 10 | Product Discovery & Sprint Planning**

Verse Companion is a minimalist writing companion designed specifically for poets.

The goal is to help poets stay in their creative flow by providing:

* 🎵 Real-time rhyme suggestions
* 🔍 Word alternatives and synonyms
* 🔢 Live syllable counting
* 💾 Automatic local poem saving
* 📱 Responsive, distraction-free writing experience

### Day 1 Focus

Today was about **product discovery and scope definition**, not coding.

I created:

* 📋 Product Requirements Document (PRD)
* 🗺️ 9-Day Implementation Blueprint
* 🎤 Project Pitch Deck

### Biggest Scoping Decision

I intentionally kept v1.0 narrow.

Instead of trying to build an AI poetry generator, meter checker, collaboration platform, and publishing system all at once, I focused on three core writing-assistance features: **rhymes, word alternatives, and syllable counting**.

The goal is simple:

> Build something genuinely useful and fully deployable in 10 days rather than something ambitious that remains unfinished.

### 10-Day Goal

By Day 10, Verse Companion should be a polished, publicly deployed v1.0 that has been tested by real users and validated as a useful writing tool.
