# Day 45 — Build an AI Decision Strategist

## Think Better. Decide Smarter.

For Day 45 of the **#60DayClaudeChallenge**, I built an **AI Decision Strategist** designed to turn a difficult decision into a structured, evidence-based decision report.

### 💡 My Decision

I used the strategist to decide whether I should:

* **Prioritize placement preparation**, or
* **Split my time equally between placements, internships, projects, and skill development.**

The AI analyzed both options using assumptions, biases, a decision matrix, a premortem, and a 7-day test plan.

### 🎯 Key Insight

The biggest insight I gained was:

> **Not every decision needs a perfect balance. Sometimes the right decision is to prioritize what is most time-sensitive while keeping the other important goal alive.**

My decision matrix favored **Placement-Prep Priority (51/70)** over **Equal-Time Split (43/70)**.

The strategist also highlighted that my real challenge wasn't choosing between skills and placements, but deciding how to **sequence my limited time effectively**.

### 🛠️ What I Built

The AI Decision Strategist includes:

* Real Decision Analysis
* Case for Each Option
* Assumption & Bias Analysis
* 7-Dimension Decision Matrix
* 12-Month Premortem
* 7-Day Test Plan
* Final Verdict
* Shareable Decision Cards
