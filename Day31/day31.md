# 🚨 AI Supply Chain Control Tower

### 60 Days Claude Challenge — Day 31

An interactive Supply Chain Operations simulation where you take the role of a **Head of Operations** and manage real-time disruptions across a global supply chain.

The objective is simple:

> Make the right operational decisions before disruptions impact your network.

---

## 🎯 Challenge

Supply chains constantly face unexpected disruptions such as:

- 🚢 Port Congestion
- 🏭 Supplier Delay
- 🚛 Truck Breakdown
- 📦 Warehouse Stockout
- 🛃 Customs Inspection
- 📈 Demand Spike
- ⚙️ Factory Machine Failure
- 🌪️ Weather Disruption
- 🔢 Wrong Inventory Count
- 📉 Damaged Shipment

Each alert requires a strategic response within a limited amount of time.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay

You play as the **Head of Operations**.

The simulation runs for **3 minutes**.

As the game progresses:

- Alerts appear dynamically
- Alert frequency increases
- Multiple disruptions can remain active
- Each decision changes business KPIs
- Delayed decisions can create future consequences
- Unresolved alerts automatically resolve negatively

The goal is to maximize your operational performance before the shift ends.

---

## 📊 Live KPIs

The dashboard tracks:

- Service Level
- Customer Satisfaction
- Inventory Health
- Transportation Efficiency
- Operating Cost
- Revenue Protected
- Score
- Remaining Time

Every decision can positively or negatively affect these metrics.

---

## ⚡ Decision System

Players can respond using actions such as:

- Expedite Shipment
- Use Backup Supplier
- Reroute Trucks
- Increase Production
- Transfer Inventory
- Approve Air Freight
- Delay Decision
- Ignore

Different actions have different business consequences.

Some actions are optimal, while others may solve an immediate problem but create additional cost or future risk.

---

## 🧠 Dynamic Difficulty

The game becomes progressively harder.

Alert spawning starts slower and accelerates as the 3-minute shift progresses.

Up to multiple alerts can remain active simultaneously, forcing the player to prioritize competing operational risks.

---

## 🏆 Performance Grading

At the end of the shift, the system calculates:

- Final Score
- Performance Grade
- Final KPI values
- Alerts Resolved
- Correct Decisions
- Wrong Decisions

Grades range from:

**A+ → A → B → C → D**

The final operational summary is generated based on overall performance.
