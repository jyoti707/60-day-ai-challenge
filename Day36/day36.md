# 🧠 Cognitive Pattern Explorer

### Day 36 — #60DayClaudeChallenge

Cognitive Pattern Explorer is a psychology-inspired, interactive self-reflection web experience designed to help users explore how they naturally approach decisions, priorities, and uncertainty.

It is designed as an **educational and reflective experience, not a psychological test or diagnosis**.

---

## 🌱 About the Project

Instead of presenting users with a traditional questionnaire, the application guides them through three interactive chapters where they can explore their thinking patterns through everyday scenarios.

The experience focuses on reflection rather than right or wrong answers.

### Thinking Tendencies

The application explores five broad thinking tendencies:

- 🔍 Analytical Thinker
- 💭 Emotional Intuitive
- 🔄 Overthinking Loop Style
- ⚡ Action-First Decision Maker
- ⚖️ Balanced Reflective Thinker

These are presented as reflective patterns rather than fixed personality labels.

---

## 🎮 Interactive Experience

### Chapter 1 — Discover Your Thinking Style

Users respond to everyday decision-making scenarios to explore how they naturally process information, uncertainty, and choices.

### Chapter 2 — Choose Your Priorities

Users arrange draggable priority cards to represent what they would focus on first in different situations.

### Chapter 3 — Map Your Thinking

Users interact with draggable timelines to visualize how their thoughts may move through a decision.

### Final Reflection — Personal Journal

The application generates a personalized reflection containing:

- Thinking pattern insights
- Percentage breakdown
- Decision-making observations
- Reflection prompts
- Personal journal space
