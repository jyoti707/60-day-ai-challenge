# 🧭 Task Compass — Startup Edition

**Learn how work flows through real organizations.**

Task Compass is an interactive, game-like workplace simulation designed to teach how **ownership, delegation, collaboration, and workflow** work inside a startup.

Instead of testing job titles or memorized concepts, the experience puts the player into realistic workplace situations and asks them to make decisions about **who should own a task, how work should move between teams, and who needs to collaborate.**

## 🎮 Gameplay

Task Compass contains three stages:

### Stage 1 — Who Owns This?

Players read realistic workplace tickets and drag the role they believe should be the **primary owner** into the ownership zone.

The game then explains:

* Primary owner
* Why they own the task
* Which roles may assist

### Stage 2 — Task Routing

Players build a workflow by arranging roles in the order that work should move through the organization.

After submission, the correct workflow is animated to show how the task travels through the company.

### Stage 3 — Collaboration Challenge

Players handle larger organizational situations by selecting up to four roles for a response team.

The debrief explains:

* Primary Owner
* Supporting Teams
* Reasoning
* Possible Communication Flow

## 🧠 Learning Objective

Task Compass is designed around one simple idea:

> Real workplace problems are rarely solved by simply finding the "right job title."

The experience encourages players to think about:

**Ownership → Delegation → Workflow → Collaboration → Escalation**
