Financial literacy

Tips, checklists & prompts to level up.

Short, student-relevant reading plus a few AI prompts you can paste into any assistant to go deeper.

01

Pay your "savings self" first

The moment allowance lands, move your savings % out before you can spend it. Automatic beats willpower.

02

Track for one week, not forever

A single honest week of logging every expense reveals 80% of your spending patterns.

03

Round-trip your wants

Wait 48 hours before non-essential purchases over 10% of your allowance. Most urges fade.

04

Name your goals

"New laptop by March" beats "save more" — specific goals with dates get funded, vague ones don't.

05

Understand compounding early

Money saved at 18 has decades to compound. Use the What-If tab to see how small % changes add up.

06

Separate spends from saves

Even a second envelope, jar, or sub-account for savings reduces accidental spending significantly.
