# Day 29 – Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab

## 🚀 Project Overview

As part of the **#60DayClaudeChallenge**, I built **Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab**, an interactive React-based simulation that allows users to experience the challenges of managing a supply chain crisis. The application is contained in a single HTML file and runs completely offline.

## ✨ Features

* Welcome screen with simulation introduction
* Random fictional company generation
* Random supply chain crisis scenarios
* War Room with strategic decision-making
* Four-round supplier negotiation simulation
* CEO Boardroom leadership assessment
* AI strategy investment selection
* Executive dashboard with business metrics and personalized feedback
* Replay functionality with randomized scenarios

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* HTML5
* CSS3
* JavaScript (ES6)
* React (CDN)
* Babel JSX

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

* Understanding supply chain risk management
* Evaluating business trade-offs during crises
* Improving negotiation and leadership decision-making
* Exploring AI applications in supply chain operations
* Building interactive React applications without a backend

## 📸 Project Assets

## 💡 Key Takeaways

* Effective crisis management requires balancing cost, speed, and customer satisfaction.
* Strong supplier relationships improve business resilience.
* AI-driven forecasting and supplier monitoring help reduce operational risks.
* Leadership decisions have a measurable impact on business performance.
